# Shared resources for /call-negotiate

Negotiation material shared with everyone who uses the skill, one Markdown file per source or topic. At the start of each call, `/call-negotiate` reads every `.md` file in this folder (except this one) alongside the [playbook](../playbook.md), and follows them where they add to or refine it.

Included:

- [general-negotiation.md](general-negotiation.md): a field guide for work, clients, vendors, boundaries and everyday disagreements.
- [salary-and-offers.md](salary-and-offers.md): a field guide for salary and job-offer negotiations: qualifying the role, a private framework, moves for common moments, package levers, and deciding.
- [tech-offers-interviewing-io.md](tech-offers-interviewing-io.md): interviewing.io's approach to tech offers: leverage and timing, what not to share, recruiter tells, countering, down-levels, and patterns at large companies.
- [fearless-salary-negotiation-doody.md](fearless-salary-negotiation-doody.md): Josh Doody's method: a walk-away number, deflecting the expectations question, a value-based counter, a planned negotiation window, and raises.
- [patrick-mckenzie-salary-negotiation.md](patrick-mckenzie-salary-negotiation.md): Patrick McKenzie's classic essay for engineers: waiting for a "yes, if", "interesting", trading new information and levers.
- [haseeb-qureshi-ten-rules.md](haseeb-qureshi-ten-rules.md): Haseeb Qureshi's ten rules for job offers: writing it down, keeping the door open, reasons, being winnable.
- [malhotra-15-rules.md](malhotra-15-rules.md): Deepak Malhotra's fifteen rules: likability, the person across the table, tough questions, the whole deal.
- [principled-negotiation.md](principled-negotiation.md): *Getting to Yes* and *Getting Past No*: interests, options, objective criteria, and handling difficult counterparts.
- [difficult-conversations.md](difficult-conversations.md): *Difficult Conversations*: intent and impact, contribution, the third story; for colleagues, family and tense moments.
- [first-offers-research.md](first-offers-research.md): what the research says about first offers, anchoring, precision, ranges and asking.

Where a source takes a different approach from the others, its file says so, and the skill offers it as an alternative line rather than picking one winner.

To add one:

- **Start the file with its source:** the book, article, video or course, with a link if there is one.
- **Write summaries in your own words.** Don't paste text from books, courses or paid newsletters.
- **Keep it short and practical:** the techniques, when to use them, and lines to adapt. The skill reads every file before each call.
- **Keep it generic.** Anything about you or a particular negotiation (your numbers, employers, walk-away, your personal style) belongs in your private folder, `~/.live-call-assistant/negotiate/` (see [prep-example.md](../prep-example.md)). Playbooks there take precedence over these.
